Privacy Policy

Corvair Companion · Last updated July 2026

Corvair Companion (“the app”) is a maintenance logbook for Chevrolet Corvair owners. This policy explains what the app collects and how that information is used.

What we collect

  • Account information — your email address, used to create and sign in to your account. If you sign in with Google, we receive your email and basic profile information from Google.
  • Your content — the vehicles, log entries, to-dos, specifications, and photos or receipts you add to the app. This is stored so it syncs across your devices.

How it's used

Your information is used solely to operate the app — to sign you in and to store and sync your garage. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or track you across other apps or websites.

Where it's stored

Data is stored using Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Cloud Storage) and processed on Google's servers as our service provider. See Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual cars, log entries, and photos directly in the app. To delete your entire account and all associated data, email support@corvaircompanion.com and we'll remove it.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time; the “last updated” date above will reflect any changes.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email support@corvaircompanion.com.
